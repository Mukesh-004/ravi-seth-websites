# Competitor review and design decisions

Reviewed on 2 October 2026. These sites are references for useful patterns, not templates to copy.

| Reference | Useful pattern | Decision for this prototype |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [R. S. Estate](https://www.ravizestate.co.in/en) | Property categories, listing cards, a multi-photo detail page, amenity icons, map and connectivity, Blogs, About, Telugu, social links and a contact form | Include those content routes and make sale/rent the first filter. Use shareable detail pages with galleries, map, icon amenities and a call request. Add social links only when real accounts are provided. |
| [MatchMyGhar](https://www.matchmyghar.com/) | Search and concrete property facts appear early; the enquiry route is obvious | Add quick browse controls in the estate hero and use direct call/visit language. Do not make unverified claims about properties. |
| [Nicobar product page](https://www.nicobar.com/products/pondicherry-kurta-black) | Size choices, material and fit information, multiple photos, and help are grouped on the product page | Add selectable sizes to boutique details and carry the chosen size into the enquiry. Leave fabric and fit fields for verified inventory data. |
| [Jaypore clothing collection](https://www.jaypore.com/c/women-clothing) | Category-led discovery and product imagery | Keep clothing central and open cars as an entirely separate visual experience. |

## Visual pass

- Replaced broad lifestyle slogans with direct language about browsing, size, price, calls and visits.
- Used the preferred modern direction for estate and boutique, with separate colour palettes. The motor site uses a light automotive system with its own navigation and layout.
- Added a complete Telugu public estate view, including property content and editorial articles. New estate entries have Telugu fields in the studio.
- Added review submission with approval before public display, a size-aware call request, a car selling call path and a shared local backend.
- Reduced duplicated product labels on cards and used size choices where they matter.
- Kept sample photography and pricing visibly identified as concept content. Real photos, measurements, availability, service area and contact details are still needed before launch.

The next design pass should use actual inventory photography. Consistent photo crop, lighting, garment angles and room views will contribute more to a distinctive identity than adding decorative effects or more fonts.
